   - Map CUDA grid/block sizes to OpenCL `global_work_size` and `local_work_size`.
   - Test different local sizes (e.g., 256, 512) for AMD optimization.

6. **Test and validate**
   - Start with a small dataset.
   - Verify correctness of the mined results.

   - Use OpenCL or CPU-side alternatives if needed.

8. **Document everything**
